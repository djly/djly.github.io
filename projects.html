Meet-GSplat

In development · v0.5

Photoreal 3D scans for car meets.

A booth-ready pipeline for turning phone photos of a car into a shareable 3D scan. Capture at the meet, process on an RTX 4090 at home, and hand the owner a branded, shareable 3D viewer — payment collected at the booth, delivery straight to their garage.

  • Node.js
  • Gaussian Splatting
  • Spark.js Viewer
  • GPU Pipeline

Volleyball Analytics Tool

Active

Turns match tape into structured data.

Processes volleyball match video through computer vision — homography, player and ball tracking — then game-logic and rally segmentation, storing the results in a Neo4j graph with a GraphQL API and a frontend for annotation-driven review.

  • Python
  • Computer Vision
  • Neo4j
  • GraphQL
