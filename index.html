About

Software by day. Slower things by night.

I'm a software developer based in Toronto, currently building at Reflexions. Before that I led technical teams through blockchain and fintech builds at Capital Blockchain, shipped design-firm platforms and cloud deployments at Minefield Consulting, and spent a few years as a research assistant at Ryerson's Ubiquitous and Pervasive Computing Lab, building apps for privacy and augmentative communication. I studied Computer Engineering at Waterloo and Computer Science at Ryerson.

Outside of that, I coached competitive gymnastics for four years, and these days I split my off hours between motorcycles, video games, volleyball, and shooting film.
