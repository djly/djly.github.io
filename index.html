About

What I do and what I like to work with.

I’m a developer with a bias towards shipping. Most of my time goes into full-stack web work: modelling the data, keeping the interface simple, and making sure the thing still feels fast once real users get to it.

I enjoy the unglamorous parts too — cleaning up state management, tightening build pipelines, and turning a spreadsheet somebody maintains by hand into a proper tool. Away from the browser I tinker with sensors and small hardware projects.
